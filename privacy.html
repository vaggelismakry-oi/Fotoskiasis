Privacy & Cookies

How fotoskiasis handles your information when you contact us or browse this site.

Who we are

fotoskiasis is an architectural lighting design studio based in Athens, Greece. For any privacy question, reach us at fotoskiasis.lighting@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you use our consultation form we collect only the details you choose to provide — your name, company or project, email, phone, areas of interest, location, project stage and message. We use these solely to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project. We do not sell them or share them with third parties.

Cookies & analytics

This site uses a small number of essential cookies needed for it to function. With your consent we also use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used so we can improve it. No analytics cookies are set until you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can decline without affecting your use of the site.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent at any time. To do so, email fotoskiasis.lighting@gmail.com.

Form delivery

Messages sent through our contact form are delivered to us by email through the form-processing service FormSubmit, transmitted securely and stored in our email inbox.
