Projects — Dionysos Zonars, Mojito Bay, Baraonda & more.

A selection of architectural lighting projects fotoskiasis has delivered in Athens and across Greece — hospitality, architectural and residential — from concept and 3D study to commissioning.

7 photos

Restaurant·Athens·Architectural Lighting

Dionysos Zonars — Ceiling of Light Over the Acropolis

A custom fibre-optic star ceiling and layered ambient scheme let the floodlit Acropolis stay the centrepiece of the view, while the dining room glows just enough to feel intimate, refined and unmistakably Athenian.
7 photos

Beach Club·Varkiza·Seaside Lighting

Mojito Bay

A seaside club lit for the long blue hour — warm uplighting threads through the palms while colour washes shift the mood across the deck, lounge beds and bar, all held below the line of the night sky.
7 photos

Restaurant·Athens·Architectural Lighting

Baraonda

An ornate Murano chandelier anchors the room while concealed cove light and candlelit tables hold a low, intimate base — the planted garden beyond kept softly luminous so the terrace feels open to the Athens night.
7 photos

Indian Restaurant·Athens·Hospitality Lighting

Tij Mahal — Restaurant Lighting

Warm cove light traces the timber roof and a backlit carved arch becomes the focal point, while coloured-glass pendants add the texture of an Indian dining room — tuned to flatter food, faces and the patterned tabletops.
7 photos

Restaurant·Plaka, Athens·Garden Lighting

Daphne's — Garden Restaurant

Wall-grazing fixtures bring out the texture of the old stone, fairy-lit palms and lanterns warm the courtyard, and every table holds its own pool of candlelight — a romantic garden that feels discovered rather than designed.
5 photos

Seaside Restaurant·Voula·Hospitality Lighting

Notos — Seaside Restaurant

Cordless table lamps and warm low-level light hold the foreground while the sea and the sunset do the rest — a terrace lit so the horizon, not the fixtures, stays the centre of attention.
7 photos

Private Residence·Naxos·Pool & Terrace Lighting

Private Villa — Naxos

Recessed wall sconces and submerged pool light turn a Cycladic terrace into a calm, sculptural night space — enough light to move and gather by, never enough to compete with the stars.
