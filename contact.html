Questions

Frequently asked.

What owners, architects and designers usually want to know before we begin.

What does a lighting study include?

A complete plan for the light of your space: the lighting concept, a layered scheme for every area, fixture selection and exact placement, technical specifications, and 3D visualisations — so you see the finished result before a single fixture is purchased.

How much does lighting design cost?

Each project is quoted individually, based on the size and complexity of the space. In practice a proper study tends to pay for itself: you buy exactly the fixtures you need, in the right quantities, and avoid costly corrections after opening.

Do you work outside Athens?

Yes. The studio is based in Athens, but we have lit projects across Greece — from the Athens Riviera to Mykonos, Milos and Naxos — and we take on international work as well.

Can you collaborate with our architect or interior designer?

Absolutely — most of our projects are collaborations. We can join the team at any stage, though the earlier light enters the conversation, the better the final result.

Do you also supply the light fixtures?

For portable and decorative lighting we are authorised dealers of Zafferano, Imagilights and Botlighting. For architectural fixtures, our study specifies exactly what to buy and where — so you purchase the right products, in the right quantity, from any supplier.

How long does a lighting study take?

It depends on the scale of the space. After a first consultation we agree a clear scope and give you a firm timeline before any work begins.
