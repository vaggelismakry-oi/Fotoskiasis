For Restaurants & Hospitality

Cordless lamps that look right on every table.

Restaurants in Greece increasingly choose rechargeable LED table lamps over candles and wired solutions — they last all night, dim smoothly, do not blow out in the meltemi wind, and never trail a cable across the floor.

The Zafferano Poldina Pro is the industry standard, but the right choice depends on table size, ceiling height, brand identity and budget. We help hospitality clients select the correct lamp, quantity and colour temperature, with volume terms for restaurant, hotel and event purchases.

Questions

Rechargeable lamps, answered.

How long does a rechargeable LED table lamp last per charge?
Most premium models such as the Zafferano Poldina Pro run for 9 hours or more on a full charge at mid brightness — enough for an entire dinner service. Many last considerably longer at lower settings.
Do you ship across Greece?
Yes. We ship rechargeable lamps across Greece — Athens, the Cyclades, the islands and beyond — as well as elsewhere in Europe on request.
Are you an authorised Zafferano dealer?
Yes. We are an authorised dealer of Zafferano, Imagilights and the other premium brands we carry, with full manufacturer warranty on every lamp.
Do you offer volume pricing for restaurants and hotels?
Yes. For hospitality clients buying multiple units we offer commercial terms and can advise on lamp selection, colour temperature and charging logistics.
Can the lamps be used outdoors?
Several models in our range are rated for outdoor use, including terraces, beachfront and pool areas. We will point you to the right option for your setting.
