Why 3D

The most expensive lighting mistakes are the ones you only see once installed.

A 3D lighting design lets us prove the scheme on screen — every fixture in place, every shadow drawn, every reflection accounted for — long before the first cable is pulled. It is the difference between approving a promise and approving a result.

For hospitality and architectural projects in particular, 3D visualization is the most cost-effective stage of a lighting study. Changes that take five minutes on screen can take days and thousands of euros on site. We render every key view — interior, exterior, by scene — until the client sees the finished room.

Each 3D lighting design is delivered alongside the full technical lighting study →.

What's Included

A complete 3D lighting package.

Photoreal night renders

Every key view of the space rendered at evening atmosphere — interior, exterior, hero shots.

Scene comparisons

Material accuracy

Renders use your real finishes — stone, timber, fabrics — so reflections and shadows read true.

Day vs. night

Tied to the study

Revision loops
