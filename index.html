The Studio

From theatre stages to the most photographed restaurants in Athens.

fotoskiasis took its first steps in 1990, designing theatrical lighting alongside leading names of the Greek and international music scene. The passion for setting colour on stages was the reason to expand into architecture — pioneering architectural lighting design in Greece at a time when the discipline barely existed here.

Three decades later, we deliver complete lighting studies for restaurants, hotels, bars, cafés, tourist resorts and private residences across Greece — supported by photoreal 3D lighting designs so the result is approved on screen, not hoped for on site.

Lighting Design in Greece

Architectural lighting design — Athens and across Greece.

fotoskiasis provides architectural lighting design in Athens and across Greece, with a portfolio that spans hospitality, residential and commercial work. Our studio delivers complete lighting studies — from initial concept and 3D lighting visualization to fixture specification and on-site commissioning.

We specialise in hotel lighting design, restaurant lighting design and bar and café lighting, as well as residential lighting design for villas in Athens, Mykonos, Santorini and the islands. Architects and interior designers partner with us as the specialist lighting consultant on their projects.

Alongside the studio, we operate a curated shop of rechargeable LED table lamps from Zafferano, Imagilights and Sister Light — the cordless lamps used on the tables of the venues we design. Whether you are planning a renovation or a new build, request a lighting study →
